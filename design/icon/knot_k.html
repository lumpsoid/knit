Knit: the knotted k

The stem is one straight strand; the second strand comes down as the arm, loops around the stem and leaves as the leg. Its three crossings alternate over and under, like a real knot, with real gaps (no painted halos), so the same strokes work on a transparent layer. Every variant shares one traced geometry; only weight, colour and finish change.
