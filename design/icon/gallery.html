Knit: app icon concepts

The idea: a tangle becomes a clear line, the way a messy ball of yarn unwinds into one straight thread. Each concept is drawn on Android's 108-unit adaptive-icon grid, with the artwork kept inside the 66-unit safe zone. Previews show a rounded-square mask, a circle mask, launcher sizes, and the icon on light and dark wallpapers. Tap ★ to mark favourites; they are highlighted on the home-screen strip at the bottom.

On a home screen

Prototype only: nothing in the app has changed. Once a concept is picked, it becomes an adaptive icon (foreground + background layers) plus the legacy PNG sizes and a monochrome layer for themed icons.
